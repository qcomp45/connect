Hermes Workspace — Privacy policy

This integration accesses only Google services and permissions authorized by its owner. Depending on those permissions and the owner's requests, it may read or modify Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Contacts data. Google data is processed by the owner's Hermes installation and may be sent to configured AI or tool providers when used in requests. Data may remain in local conversation history, logs, and files until the owner deletes it. OAuth credentials are stored separately from this public website. This website does not receive Google account data or OAuth credentials and adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, or third-party scripts. GitHub may collect ordinary hosting request information under its privacy statement. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections; revoking access does not delete previously stored copies. This integration does not sell Google user data or use it to train generalized AI models. Use of Google API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. For support, use the developer email displayed on the Google consent screen.
